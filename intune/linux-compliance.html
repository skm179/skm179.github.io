Linux / Ubuntu Compliance Lab Notes

This lab documents the process of configuring and validating device encryption compliance for an Ubuntu device enrolled in Microsoft Intune.

Compliance lifecycle: Compliant → Policy Applied → Noncompliant → Encryption Remediated → Compliant

1. Initial Device State

The Ubuntu device was initially enrolled in Intune and reported as compliant.

2. Create the Encryption Compliance Policy

A Linux compliance policy named Linux-Device Encryption was created in Intune.

The policy included the requirement: Require Device Encryption .

3. Device Becomes Noncompliant

After the encryption requirement was applied, the Ubuntu device did not satisfy the requirement and was reported as noncompliant.

4. Identify the Compliance Issue

The Ubuntu device displayed a compliance warning indicating that the device did not meet the organization's security and compliance requirements.

The specific issue identified was the requirement to encrypt the device's storage.

5. Remediation

The device encryption requirement was addressed through the Linux environment and Intune Agent.

6. Verify Compliance

After remediation, the compliance policy was checked again. The encryption requirement changed to Compliant.

The Linux device was then verified in Intune and returned to a compliant state.

Final result: The Ubuntu device successfully met the configured device encryption compliance requirement and was reported as compliant in Microsoft Intune.
